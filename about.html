Methodology

The 5-Stage Precision Execution Workflow

From initial geological selection to lifetime maintenance handover, each milestone follows documented ANSI and TCNA quality assurance protocols.

01

Substrate Analysis

Moisture testing (ASTM F2170), joist spacing audits, and L/720 deflection calculations to eliminate fracture hazards before stone arrives.

02

3D Laser Templating

High-resolution optical laser measurements capture every millimetric contour, projecting exact vein alignment across walls and waterfalls.

03

CNC Waterjet Cutting

Diamond abrasive waterjets execute chip-free 45° miter drops, integral undermount sink bevels, and curbless trench drainage slopes.

04

Mechanical Setting

Polymer-modified ANSI A118.15 thinset mortar paired with precision screw-leveling anchors guarantees completely flat, zero-lippage seams across every joint.

05

Micro-Hone & Seal

Planetary diamond buffing up to 3,000-grit optical mirror clarity, sealed with deep-penetrating oleophobic fluoropolymer acid barrier.
