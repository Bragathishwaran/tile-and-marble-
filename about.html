Who We Are

Where Geological Beauty Meets Engineered Precision

ServicePro began as a small boutique workshop in 1998, hand-finishing Carrara marble mantels and private Italian tile foyers. Over the past quarter century, we have grown into the metropolitan area's most trusted contractor for high-profile residential estates, luxury hotels, and commercial flagship properties.

Direct Quarry Sourcing

We inspect and hand-select raw marble and granite slabs directly from Italian and Spanish quarries.

Zero-Lippage System

Patented mechanical leveling clips guarantee 100% flat seam transitions even on 5ft x 10ft porcelain slabs.

Our Journey

Timeline of Growth & Milestones

From our initial artisan workshop to handling metropolitan high-rise contracts, explore the milestones that defined our reputation.

1998

Founding of ServicePro Studio

Master cutter Matteo Rossi arrives from Tuscany and establishes a bespoke tile and hand-honed stone restoration studio in New York.

2007

NTCA Five-Star Contractor Accreditation

Achieved formal certification from the National Tile Contractors Association, standardizing our ANSI A108 subfloor installation protocols.

2016

Launch of 100% Dustless Diamond Grinding

Invested $1.2M in specialized HEPA wet-vacuum diamond grinding equipment, enabling luxury residential restorations without vacating the home.

Present Day

1.8 Million Sq Ft Milestone

Celebrating over 3,400 completed architectural installations and the grand opening of our 15,000 sq ft live stone slab gallery.
