How We Work

The 4-Step Master Installation Roadmap

Why our stone floors never crack, hollow, or lift. Our strict adherence to TCNA engineering protocols guarantees generational durability.

01

Subfloor Profiling

We test subfloor moisture (RH%), remove deflection, and pour self-leveling underlayment to achieve a 1/8" flatness per 10 feet.

02

Uncoupling Membrane

Installation of Schluter-DITRA waterproofing and vapor management membranes to neutralize substrate stress cracks.

03

Full-Coverage Thinset

100% back-buttering on all natural stone slabs with polymer-modified mortar, preventing any hollow pockets or impact shattering.

04

Diamond Buff & Seal

Epoxy grout injection, diamond grit planar buffing, and multi-coat nano-penetrating fluoropolymer impregnating sealant.
